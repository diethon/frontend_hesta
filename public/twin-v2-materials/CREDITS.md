# Material microdetail

Normal and roughness maps (1K JPEG) from Poly Haven:

- **Wood Table 001** — https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_table_001
- **Denim Fabric** — https://polyhaven.com/a/denim_fabric

License: **CC0 1.0**, as published at https://polyhaven.com/license.
Download source URLs, file sizes and original MD5 hashes are recorded in `sources.json`.

These files are stored locally and used only as PBR surface microdetail on actual
Three.js geometry. They do not replace any room, furniture or interactive model.
Color, plaster and stone surface maps are generated procedurally in `surfaces.tsx`.
