## NeonPlan 3D

Renderer geometry adapted from https://github.com/Mastershort/neonplan3d, commit `bb5261d29fc45ba0bb4a586bca2500f94ec9ea1d`.

Ported files under `src/components/twin/neonplan/`: shared wall generation, polygon/slab triangulation, fold shader, opening parts, built-in procedural furniture and lamps, procedural floor-pattern atlas/shader, heatmap colour interpolation and geometry utilities. HESTA supplies its own view models, Redux state, REST commands, WebSocket integration and light materials. No external furniture packs, Pro assets or Home Assistant integration are included. The reference screenshot in `docs/evidence/neonplan-port/neonplan-day-reference.jpg` comes from the same MIT repository's `docs/images/view-day.jpg`.
